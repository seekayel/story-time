# Captain Barba y Chuck — Creative Brief

## Premise

Captain Barba and his monkey companion Chuck have adventures across the Caribbean while trying to clear the captain's name.

## Audience

Children ages 6–10. Episodes should feel adventurous, funny, emotionally clear, and exciting without explicit violence or adult content.

## Creative ingredients

- Genre: children's serialized adventure comedy
- Setting: the Caribbean, centered on a pirate ship and island communities
- Themes: friendship, reputation, courage, ingenuity, making amends
- Main characters: Captain Barba, Chuck, and a narrator
- Comparable works: not yet specified

## Format

- Episodes per season: 24
- Episode length: 25 minutes
- Continuity: each episode stands alone for a new listener and moves the season arc forward
- Source language: English (`script.md`)
- Release language: Spanish (`script-es.md`)
- Narration: yes
- Content boundaries: no explicit violence or adult content; legacy rating was PG-13
- Production: dialogue-forward audio with ElevenLabs voices
